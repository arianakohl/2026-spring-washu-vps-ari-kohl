I personally prefer to do things the slow and hard way in order to feel most proud of my work and results, however if I have a deadline on something this oftentimes is not feasible. With the way school assignment deadlines function it makes it really difficult to decide to opt out of timeliness and efficiency as the foremost priorities. Students have tons of balls in the air to juggle so I usually will favor efficiency rather than absolute craftsmanship at times. In my personal life, though, I am always one to cherish craftsmanship and effort above all. I feel that projects do not feel fulfilling and satisfying if you did not go about their completion in the proper way. Take the time, expend your energy, and work hard and your successful results will feel that much better and more gratifying because you will have truly earned it. Also, when you know that your efforts were an integral part of getting the job done and you understand your role in that - I feel that we begin to trust ourselves and our capacity for growth more. The best way to achieve a goal usually isn’t the easiest way for a reason.
