This fall I attended the St. Louis Renaissance Faire for the very first time. It was so incredible and transformative for me, like going through a time portal to a lost culture. What I found to be so captivating was the immersion of it all with being deep inside the woods. All these small booths and huts with Renaissance-themed activities, food, and more were built within the forest - not in place of it. Something I miss from the past that I never got to experience was the time period where human beings respected nature more and appreciated being one with the natural world and animals. I feel we were meant to live within the nature world (just as the Renaissance Faire was constructed) rather than obliterating it to make space for ourselves. We should have evolved to appreciate the natural beauty of our planet more I think and learned to live in harmony with it, disturbing it as little as possible just like how all other animal species on this planet live. Why are we the only species that deviated from this mindset? I don’t know but I wish we hadn’t.
