I really enjoyed the argument put forth in this essay that "the cloud" has a detrimental and exclusionary connotation for what the world wide web actually is. This metaphor makes the world wide web feel inaccessible and tricks everyday artists/people into believing they can't effectively contribute to it. This essay reminded me of the true purpose of the web and that we must not forget each of our individual contributions are just as relevant and real as the big website giants like Google or Facebook or Instagram. Everything when it comes down to it is just another URL. Therefore, we should assert our power and capitilize on our potential to make the web a creative and expressive space, filled to the brim with the unique signatures of each human. We can do better than allowing these big corporates to monopolize our freedom of expression.
