I particularly liked the part of Eno where Brian Eno discusses environmentalism- a topic that I care deeply about. He quotes English environmentalist, Tom Rivett Carnack. He said, “we always overestimate what we can do in a day and underestimate what we will do in 10 years.” I agree with Brian Eno that this is en encouraging thought because if we commit ourselves to leveraging our technology and sense of justice to start looking after nature instead of being ignorant to it, We Can. Do. It. I found Brian Eno to be incredibly wise and introspective; he inspired me to try taking a step out of the day-to-day autopilot, and to be intentional in giving permission to my own imagination. I love the film’s dedication to promoting creativity and designing conditions in your life, and the world around you, to allow for creativity to blossom. Brian Eno’s way of thinking about creativity correlates with his passion for the environment because both things are predicated upon interacting systems and generative, collaborative efforts.
