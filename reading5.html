I really liked this article and morality it presented about the progression of technology. I thought it was a really interesting way to look at “ideal” technological advancement as a style where technology doesn’t assert itself to be interrupt our lives, but rather quietly exists in the periphery to help us assist and optimize our life. A lot of current technology goes against this principle, like emails, social media, and push notifications that ask us loudly to pay attention to them. Preferred calm technology is ambient and non-intrusive for our attention spans. Calm technology helps us perceive more of the world without blatantly distracting us or needing to grab our attention. I am in agreement with the authors that this should be the bar we strive for when considering technological development and is, in my opinion, an appropriate and reasonable request. I also made a connection between this article and Super Normal, the book I focused my last website on. Super Normal also rewards technology and invention that function to quietly assist us, not to jump in our faces and interrupt/distract us from living our lives.
