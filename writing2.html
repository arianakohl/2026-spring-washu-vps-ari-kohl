A human-made creation that brings out the best in me is the sketch comedy club here at WashU called Kids on Campus. The club was created just over 10 ten years ago by WashU students as a collegiate version of Saturday Night Live. I was part of my high school theater group which really gave me a sense of community and my lasting friendships from my hometown. When I came to college I didn’t really feel drawn to the drama clubs here despite really wanting to find a similar community-esque outlet to fulfill this desire for a sense of belonging to something here at college. I struggled to find this group for a while and tried joining many different extracurriculars during my first few semesters at WashU. I couldn't find one that gave me that same sense of community and “my people” that I was really searching for her. At the beginning of my sophomore spring semester I joined Kids on Campus and my entire college experience totally transformed. I could tell nearly straight away that this was the place to me and where I belonged at WashU. It was so invigorating to finally find a group of like-minded people where I really felt like an integral member of the group. It was also so refreshing to find that group that worked for me where everyone was additionally equally passionate at working toward a shared goal – our sketch comedy performance at the end of each semester. This culmination mixed with all of our social chemistry was exactly what I had been searching for out of my college experience and everyone I have bonded with there has truly changed me and my life for the better.
